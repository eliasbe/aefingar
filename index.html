Injury swap notes
  • Fibula fracture (~4 months post): no impact — bike/row/ski for all conditioning, no jumping or running intervals. Load squats and hinges only through pain-free ankle range (box squat, elevated heels, trap bar). Progressive calf raises are rehab, not a risk. Defer to the physio on anything ankle-near-the-floor.
  • Climbing finger: protect from crimp-like loading — straps or open-hand/hook grip on deadlifts, rows, pull-ups and carries. Band-assisted pull-ups reduce grip demand. Skip anything loading the digit in isolation.
  • Knee history: pick knee angles that are pain-free that day — box squats, step-ups and hip-dominant variants substitute freely. Reverse lunges over forward. Warm the knee up properly before Slot 1; never train through joint pain.
Progression rules
  • Double progression: stay at a weight until you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, then add 2.5–5 kg and rebuild from the bottom.
  • Effort: leave 1–2 reps in the tank (RPE 7–8); the main lift may touch RPE 8–9 on the last set.
  • Swapping is a feature — but keep one main-lift choice for 3–4 weeks so progression is measurable.
  • Never train through joint pain — swap the slot to a pain-free variant instead.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks, or when everything feels heavy: one week at ~60 %.
Scheduling · alternate A and B regardless of calendar gaps — whichever you did last, do the other next. 2×/wk: AB AB · 3×/wk: ABA BAB.
If pressed for time · drop the finisher, then slots 4+5, then trim 2+3 to 2 sets. Slot 1 is never skipped.
